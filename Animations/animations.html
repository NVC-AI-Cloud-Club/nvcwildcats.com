04 / BEFORE THE VAULT

THE EVOLUTION
OF AI.

The current wave of generative AI is part of a much longer story. These milestones provide context for the technology students are exploring today.

1950s

The Question

Researchers began formally exploring whether machines could demonstrate forms of intelligent behavior.

1980s–90s

Machine Learning

Statistical methods allowed computers to learn patterns from increasingly large collections of data.

2000s

Data at Scale

Powerful processors, cloud computing, and enormous datasets accelerated the practical development of modern AI systems.

TODAY

Generative AI

Modern systems can generate text, images, audio, code, and video, opening new possibilities for creative experimentation.
