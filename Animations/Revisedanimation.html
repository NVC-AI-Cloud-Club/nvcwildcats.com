1980s: The Age of the Script

In the 1980s, "Artificial Intelligence" was mostly an illusion created by clever math. Consoles like the Atari 2600 had so little memory that enemies couldn't "think"—they followed rigid, hard-coded scripts.

Source: Wikipedia

1990s: Getting Clever with 3D

Titles like Quake introduced Finite State Machines (FSM). For the first time, an enemy could have a "state of mind."

Source: Wikipedia

2000s: The Era of Suggestion

The Netflix Prize proved that AI could understand human behavior better than humans could using Collaborative Filtering.

Source: Wikipedia

Today: The AI Brain

Entered the age of Generative Intelligence.Deep learning is a type of machine learning that uses neural networks with many layers to recognize patterns, learn from data, and make decisions. In video games, deep learning is used to create more intelligent and realistic behaviors, such as advanced non-player characters (NPCs), adaptive difficulty, and realistic animations. It can also help generate game content, improve graphics (like upscaling or rendering), and personalize player experiences by learning how individuals play..
